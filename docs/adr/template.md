## Decision
The decision taken described in a few sentences, frequently bolded or italicized so it stands out. (It tells you which option was
selected during the decision taking but nothing more: doesn’t include: neither the problem it solves,
nor any mention of the options that didn’t make the cut, nor the consequences of the selected option and why it was selected.)

## Context
The Context section provides the facts surrounding and driving the decision as they were known to the decider or deciders and the advisers at the time.
A Context section tells the reader why the decision matters and gives an idea of the circumstances thatwere on the mind of the person or team doing the deciding.
Includes: Decision Criteria & Example


## Options
Options section details the options considered during the
decision process. It is important that this section details not only the decision path taken but also the paths considered but not taken. 
Having only one option is a bad smell in ADRs because there is always more than one option in a decision.

Other Notes: Options are numbered, short, the 1st one is traditionally the choosen one

## Consequences
The Consequences section details the reasons why the selected option was chosen and why the other options were not.

Other Notes: Options are numbered as in last paragraph,pro & contra, why selected/ why NOT


## Advices (Optional)
Advice, is a simple bulleted list of contributions from
advice offerers.6 I’ve found that it helps to have the advice in the format [Advice]
([Advice-giver’s name, role]) with some idea of the date it was offered.
